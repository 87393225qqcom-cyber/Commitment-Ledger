Commitment Ledger: quick manual承诺账本 · 简明手册

Every day has three steps: Commit in the morning, Work, then Close the day.每天三步：早上「承诺」，然后「工作」，晚上「结束今天」。

1. Install on your phone1. 安装到手机

  • iPhone: open the app link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome → ⋮ → Install app.
  • Always open it from the Home Screen icon. Updates install themselves; to get one, close the app fully and reopen it.
  • iPhone：用 Safari 打开应用链接 → 分享 → 添加到主屏幕。
  • 安卓：用 Chrome 打开 → ⋮ → 安装应用。
  • 请始终从主屏幕图标打开。更新会自动生效，完全关闭应用再重新打开即可。

2. Projects and tasks2. 项目与任务

  • Projects tab → name a project and pick a category: Passion-driven, Must-do or Baseline.
  • Choose No deadline or Has a deadline, and a tracking type: Word count (writing), Time (workouts, practice) or Other (milestones, e.g. 3D work).
  • Add tasks under each project. Tap To today to put one on today's list.
  • 在 Projects 页新建项目，选择类别：热爱驱动、必须完成、日常基础。
  • 选择无截止日期或有截止日期，以及记录方式：字数（写作）、时间（运动、练习）或其他（里程碑，如 3D 建模）。
  • 在项目下添加任务，点 To today 加入今天的计划。

3. Today: commit, work, close3. 今天：承诺、工作、结束

  1. Commit: add 1–3 concrete outcomes, then Lock in. After that, tasks can be checked off or dropped with a reason, never quietly deleted.
  2. Work: press Focus on a task, and log words, minutes or a note under Log progress.
  3. Close the day: rate the day, tag what got in the way, write the first task for tomorrow, then tap Close the day.
  1. 承诺：写下 1–3 个具体成果，点 Lock in 锁定。锁定后任务只能完成或注明原因放弃，不能悄悄删除。
  2. 工作：在任务上点 Focus 开始专注；在 Log progress 记录字数、分钟或完成说明。
  3. 结束今天：给今天打分、标记干扰因素、写下明天第一件事，然后点 Close the day。

Streak: a day counts when you close it having kept at least half your commitments. Rest days don't break it.连续天数：结束当天且完成至少一半承诺即计入；休息日不会中断。

4. Focus timer4. 专注计时器

  • Press Focus to open the full-screen timer. Pick a target: 15m, 25m, 50m or 1h 30m.
  • The outer ring fades away as time passes. The black dot marks now.
  • When time is up, a sound plays: choose +10 min, Keep going or Stop and log. Change the sound in Settings.
  • On iPhone, turn the ringer on and keep the app open on screen. If the screen still sleeps, set Auto-Lock to Never during focus.
  • 点 Focus 打开全屏计时器，选择目标时长：15、25、50 分钟或 1.5 小时。
  • 外圈刻度随时间逐渐消失，黑点表示当前时刻。
  • 时间到会播放提示音，可选 +10 min、Keep going（继续）或 Stop and log（停止并记录）。提示音可在 Settings 中更换。
  • iPhone 请关闭静音模式并让应用保持在前台；如果屏幕仍会熄灭，专注时可将「自动锁定」设为「永不」。

5. Back up your data5. 备份数据

  • Your data is stored only on this phone. Nothing goes to a server.
  • Once a week: Settings → Export data (JSON) → Save to Files / iCloud Drive.
  • To restore: Settings → Import data (JSON).
  • ⚠️ Deleting the app icon, clearing Safari website data or changing the app link removes your data. Export first.
  • 数据只保存在本机，不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 每周一次：Settings → Export data (JSON) → 存储到「文件」或 iCloud 云盘。
  • 恢复：Settings → Import data (JSON)。
  • ⚠️ 删除主屏幕图标、清除 Safari 网站数据或更换应用链接都会丢失数据，请先导出备份。
